# AI News Radar

## Product

AI News Radar is a public, static research dashboard that turns a broad set of AI sources into a focused 24-hour signal brief. Readers use it to scan important model, product, developer, industry, research, community, and creator updates without maintaining their own feed pipeline.

## Users and purpose

- Primary users: AI researchers, investors, builders, and enthusiasts who want a fast, trustworthy daily scan.
- Maintainers: people who fork the repository and connect their own public or secret-backed sources.
- Core task: understand what changed, why it matters, and which topics or model capabilities deserve follow-up.

## Experience principles

- Keep the default reading path focused; advanced source controls remain secondary.
- Make provenance, freshness, and source health visible.
- Prefer compact research-oriented layouts over promotional presentation.
- Preserve access on phones, tablets, and desktops; core content must never depend on hover.
- Attribute external benchmark data and link readers to the original source.

## Visual direction

- Register: product
- Existing identity: restrained warm-neutral surfaces, dark editorial type, teal accent, compact square-cornered controls.
- Density: information-rich but scannable, with clear hierarchy and minimal decoration.
- Motion: functional state feedback only; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Anti-references

- Do not turn the page into a marketing landing page or a grid of decorative cards.
- Do not add reader-facing configuration when a good default works.
- Do not use gradients, glass effects, oversized rounded containers, or ornamental animation.
- Do not publish unaudited private feeds, credentials, or copied proprietary datasets.
